Problem Statement

Food delivery businesses generate large amounts of order data, but raw data alone does not clearly show customer preferences, demand patterns, revenue behaviour, or delivery performance.

This project transforms food ordering data into interactive Tableau visualizations that help identify meaningful customer and operational patterns across metropolitan cities, meal windows, and dining categories.

Verified Key Insights

Core empirical findings extracted directly from the verified 50,000-record Tableau dataset.

Bangalore Leads Total Order Activity

Why it matters: Bangalore records the highest transaction volume, closely trailed by Delhi and Mumbai. Rider fleet allocation and cloud kitchen partnerships yield maximum ROI in these core metros.

Indian Regional Cuisines Dominate Revenue

Why it matters: North Indian and South Indian cuisines generate the majority of total platform revenue across all 6 cities. Menu curation and promotional combos must prioritize regional staples.

Dinner & Lunch Anchor Platform GMV

Why it matters: Dinner represents the single largest revenue contributor, with Lunch as the secondary peak. Premium delivery slots and promotional campaigns are best monetized during these peak windows.

Working Adults Drive Core Order Volume

Why it matters: Working adults and mature consumers (ages 21–40+) generate the core volume of orders and show higher basket sizes in Fast Casual and Fine Dining segments.

Corporate Orders Peak During Midday

Why it matters: Office and corporate catering orders surge predictably during lunch hours with larger basket sizes, pointing toward high-potential B2B team lunch subscriptions.

Delivery Durations Exhibit Operational Consistency

Why it matters: Delivery durations cluster tightly around operational SLA norms with minimal tail delays, directly correlating with sustained positive customer satisfaction ratings.
