*(Action: Scrolling down to the Problem Statement and Project Objectives sections)*

> *Without structured analytics, stakeholders struggle to forecast meal surges, allocate driver fleets, or customize marketing promotions.*  
> *To solve this, our project defines six focused objectives:*  
> *First, understand customer ordering behavior across group contexts.*  
> *Second, identify dominant cuisines.*

> *Fifth, quantify revenue contributions by meal category.*  
> *And sixth, evaluate delivery fulfillment transit performance."*

### 01:10–01:50 | Dataset & Quality Audit
*(Action: Scrolling to the Project Snapshot and Dataset characteristics)*
> *"Let us examine the underlying data. This project analyzes a verified dataset containing exactly 50,000 transaction records across 19 distinct attributes.*  
> *Key dimensions include customer identifiers, metropolitan cities—specifically Bangalore, Delhi, Mumbai, Hyderabad, Pune, and Chennai—cuisine categories, meal classifications, restaurant formats, and customer company contexts.*  
> *Key quantitative measures include gross order value in Indian Rupees, delivery transit fees, fulfillment time in minutes, customer ratings from one to five stars, and customer age.*  
> *Data integrity was strictly validated: row count verification confirmed all 50,000 records, key dimensions contained zero null values, and the Tableau Hyper extract matches our verified baseline checksum bit-for-bit."*
